THE BRAND LEGACY

Not Becoming Someone New. Remembering Who You Are.

Veronica O’Donnell

Founder, Quiet Power Keynote Speaker & Mindset Coach

“ Your problem isn’t a lack of power. It’s that you’ve lost full access to it.

I discovered this after waking from a three-day coma. Because it didn’t matter how strong I was, or how capable I was I was disconnected. I didn’t need an intensive care unit to tell me that.

Without taking control of my thoughts, my feelings, and my actions, I couldn’t access the power that had been there all along. So I took ownership and organised what I learned into the Quiet Power System: a practical decision-making method that helps people navigate life with more power, more control, and more freedom.
